About Khanqah-e-Saqlainia Sharafatiya

۞
خانقاہِ عالیہ شرافتیہ، شاہ آباد، بریلی شریف

Established on 1st January, 1950 by our revered patron saint, Hazrat Qiblah Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan Radiallahu Anhu in Mohalla Shahbad, Bareilly Sharif, Khanqah-e-Saqlainia Sharafatiya stands as a sanctuary of spiritual tranquility, unyielding adherence to the Sunnah, and noble service to humanity.

Foundation & Historical Evolution

Following decades of rigorous spiritual asceticism (Muhasiba, Mujahida, Muraqiba, and Mushahida), and after receiving Khilafah and Trusteeship in 1936 from his spiritual mentor Hazrat Qiblah Shah Muhammad Basheer Miyan R.h., Hazrat Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan founded this sacred Markaz to kindle the divine light of Tasawwuf in the hearts of common seekers.

The Khanqah has been sanctified through the footsteps of two greatest saintly luminaries: Hazrat Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan R.h., his beloved son Maulana Sufi Shah Shuja'at Ali Miyan R.h., and his venerable grandson and Sajjada Nasheen, Shaykh Muhammad Saqlain Miyan R.h., whose charisma brought millions of devotees into the fold of piety.

Spiritual Pillars & Traditions

1. Halqa-e-Dhikr

Regular assemblies of silent and audible dhikr following the Quadriyah and Mujaddidiyah traditions to cultivate pure remembrance of Allah Almighty.

2. Langar-e-Aam

Daily distribution of blessed food to hundreds of visitors, wayfarers, and local poor, perpetuating the centuries-old Sufi hospitality.

3. Universal Brotherhood

Welcoming seekers irrespective of caste, ethnicity, or background, fostering social harmony and communal peace throughout India.

4. Academic & Ethical Guidance

Publishing spiritual booklets, offering moral arbitration, and organizing philanthropic drives under Hazrat Shah Saqlain Academy.
