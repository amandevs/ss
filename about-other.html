Public Welfare & Humanitarian Initiatives

۞

"The best among you are those who bring greatest benefit to humankind." Following this prophetic guidance, Khanqah-e-Sharafatiya engages in widespread social, medical, and educational initiatives.

🍲

The Blessed Langar Khana

Operating non-stop throughout the year, the Langar Khana prepares fresh, nutritious meals served freely to all visitors, devotees, traveling caravans, and impoverished local residents without discrimination.

🏥

Free Medical & Health Camps

Regular health checkups, blood donation camps, eye screening drives, and free medicine distribution are organized in collaboration with volunteer doctors during Urs Mubarak and key commemorative dates.

📚

Hazrat Shah Saqlain Academy

Empowering youth through moral education, scholarships for deserving students, career counseling, library resources, and publishing classical texts on Islamic spirituality and communal amity.

🤝

Pilgrim Caravans Coordination

Arranging smooth logistics, reception committees, and lodging at Bareilly Junction and throughout the city for caravans traveling from Mumbai, Bhopal, Gwalior, Gujarat, and across Uttar Pradesh.
