Hazrat Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan R.h.
أَلَا إِنَّ أَوْلِيَاءَ اللَّهِ لَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ

"AL QURA’AN : Beware ! Verily, the friends of Allah will not have any fear, nor will they grieve"

Surah Yunus (10:62)

Prophetic Declaration on the Awliya: Imam Nawawi Radiallahu Anhu and also Ibn Majah Radiallahu Anhu report that when the Holy Prophet Muhammad Swallallahu Alaihi Wa Aalihi Sallam was asked about the characteristics of the Awliya (the caring friends of Allah), he declared:

‘Allah is remembered when they are seen.’

Hence, if one finds that the company of a pious person generates the thought and love of Allah in your heart, then he is certainly a Wali (caring friend of Allah). When a true friend discards his qualities and existence for the majestic qualities and divine existence of Almighty Allah, his heart never ever indulges in worldly desires.

Biography & Spiritual Journey

Hazrat Qiblah Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan Radiallahu Anhu

Patron Saint of Silsila-e-Aaliyah Sharafatiya Mujaddidiya Quadriyah

True to this description, the patron saint of Silsila e Aaliyah Sharafatiya Mujaddidiya Quadriyah, Hazrat Qiblah Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan Radiallahu Anhu, through his valued teachings and radiance of uloom e zaahir o baatin (outer and inner knowledge), propagated the true meaning of Tasawwuf, which implies ultimate and definite belief in Allah and complete submission to His divine will.

Blessed Birth & Pious Family

Born on the 27th day of the sacred month of Ramadan in Hijri 1310, corresponding to 29th April, 1892, in the blessed moments of Adhan e Saani of Jumu'atul Wada, to a fasting, blessed, pious mother Hameeda Khatoon Rahmatullahi Alaiha, our patron saint came to this world through a family that reached heights of piety, humbleness, devotion and closeness to Allah.

Being an educated and elite zamindar family of Kakrala, Badayun Sharif, this family were referred to for all problems and queries, and presided over arbitration for all issues in the locality. But the elite background was never an impediment to their utmost piety and devotion to Allah and His cause.

The patron saint’s father Hazrat Ghulam Mustafa Khan Rahmatullahi Alaih was a sincere, humble, pious friend of Allah and extremely punctual on prescribed timings of worship. The patron saint’s maternal grandfather Hazrat Ghulam Yaseen Khan was the Khatib and Imaam of the ancient Jame Masjid of Kakrala, and a person who never missed his takbeer e oola of Salaah in Jamaat nor did he ever miss his tahajjud prayers for forty years. Such was the pious nature of the family of our patron saint.

Education & Exemplary Humility

Upon completion of his preliminary/primary education at home, Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan Radiallahu Anhu joined a local institution for further studies from where he passed out in the year 1911 with top grades and then began teaching in the same institute.

His caring, humble nature and prideless attitude was evident from the fact that he made arrangements for ablution (Wudu) in the Masjid and personally served the worshippers with water and towels. His sincere and untiring efforts of Muhasiba, Mujahida, Muraqiba and Mushahida continued unceasingly.

Spiritual Master & Initiation (1926)

In his search for a true spiritual guide, he was blessed by Allah Subhanahu Ta’ala to meet a towering master saint of that era: Hazrat Qiblah Shah Muhammad Basheer Miyan Radiallahu Anhu as his master and spiritual guide in June 1926, and the radiance of inner enlightenment began with the sense of Divine taste and satisfaction in his spiritual endeavors.

Hazrat Qiblah Shah Muhammad Basheer Miyan Radiallahu Anhu was born in 1859 in Khurasan, Iran and travelled to erstwhile Hind for further enlightenment and later camped in Bareilly Sharif in his own Khanqaah. Hazrat Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan Radiallahu Anhu resigned from his teaching obligations in May 1935 and began spending his life in the sohbah (intimate companionship) of his master in Bareilly.

In 1936, his Murshid offered him Khilafah and Ijazah and made him the sole Trustee of the Khanqaah.

Foundation of Khanqaah in Shahbad (1950)

Upon his Murshid’s demise, our patron saint established his own Khanqaah on 1st January, 1950 in Mohalla Shahbad, Bareilly Sharif. His way of guidance was strictly aligned to the instruction in the Holy Qura’an of inviting towards the path of Allah with wisdom and graceful counsel.

At the age of 65, he got engrossed and immersed completely into deep divine meditation, secluding himself from all worldly affairs and spending his remaining days in constant Dhikrullah and spiritual communion.

Khilafat & Illustrious Successors

Hazrat Shah Sharafat Ali Miyan Radiallahu Anhu accorded Khilafat and Ijazah to the following fortunate persons:

1. His son: Maulana Sufi Shah Shuja'at Ali Miyan Alaihirrahma (Kakrala Sharif)

2. His grandson and Custodian: Shaykh Muhammad Saqlain Miyan Radiallahu Anhu (Bareilly Sharif)
